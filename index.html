01 In the browser

GPay Activity Studio

Drop a Google Takeout My Activity.html export, or a CSV this page already extracted, to see spend, transfers, merchants, and trends. The steps to download the right file are below.

A browser tool by Akshay Biradar

Privacy

Nothing from your file is saved. It stays in this browser tab. It is never uploaded, and it is not written to cookies, local storage, or a server.

Parsing and charts run on your device. Refreshing or closing the tab clears the data. A CSV download is a file you choose to save yourself. The chart library loads from a public CDN. Your transactions are not sent with that request.

Page visits are counted without cookies, using the same Cloudflare and Umami tools as biradarakshay.com. That count is the page address only. It does not include your file, its name, merchants, or amounts.

How to get My Activity.html

This page reads the HTML history from Google Takeout’s My Activity product, filtered to Google Pay. A statement from the GPay app, a bank CSV, or the separate Google Pay product download (usually JSON) will not work.

  1. 1

    On a computer, open takeout.google.com and sign in with the Google account you use for Google Pay.

  2. 2

    Under “Select data to include”, click Deselect all so every product is turned off.

  3. 3

    Scroll to My Activity and turn on only that product. Leave the separate Google Pay and Google Wallet products off. Those exports use a different format, and this page cannot read them.

  4. 4

    Under My Activity, click All activity data included. The button sometimes says “Multiple formats”.

  5. 5

    Click Deselect all again, then select only Google Pay. Click OK.

  6. 6

    At the bottom of the page, click Next step.

  7. 7

    Set delivery to Send download link via email, frequency to Export once, and file type to .zip. Click Create export.

  8. 8

    Wait for Google’s email. It often arrives in minutes and can take several hours. Open Download your files and save the archive. The link usually expires after about 7 days.

  9. 9

    Unzip the archive. The file you want is Takeout/My Activity/Google Pay/My Activity.html.

  10. 10

    Drop that file below, or use Choose file. A CSV you previously downloaded from this page works too.

Make sure it is the Google Pay file

The zip can contain several files named My Activity.html (Search, YouTube, Maps, and others). Use only the one inside the Google Pay folder. Opened in a browser, it should list payments such as Paid, Sent, and Received, each with a date.

A large history is sometimes split into My Activity.html, My Activity-1.html, and so on in that same folder. Load each part on its own; this page does not merge files.

If the Google Pay folder contains only JSON, you exported the Google Pay product instead of My Activity. Start again from step 2 and select My Activity only.

    Nothing from your activity file is uploaded or saved. HTML parsing looks for repeating outer-cell blocks (Google Pay My Activity format). Categories are name-based heuristics and will mislabel some merchants. Google Pay exports are not a full bank ledger — salary, cash, and other UPI apps may be missing.
